ABOUT USBOOTH

Distance shouldn't
own the memory.

UsBooth is a digital photobooth built around a simple idea: make it easy to create a keepsake with the people who matter — whether they're sitting beside you or somewhere else in the world.

Built for the moment

UsBooth combines camera sharing, synchronized shooting, creative layouts and memory tools into one playful experience.

Free at the core

The goal is to keep the core experience accessible. Future partnerships, event experiences and sponsored creative themes can support the product without putting the basic booth behind a paywall.

Privacy matters

Temporary sharing and permanent Memory Vault storage are separate concepts. We want users to know exactly where a photo lives and how long a shared link lasts.
